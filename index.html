Minecraft

Minecraft has 3 Dimensions.
  • Overworld
  • Nether
  • End
Minecraft also has 4 Game Modes.
  • Survival
  • Creative
  • Adventure
  • Spectator
There are also 4 Main Bosses in Minecraft.
  • Ender Dragon
  • Wither
  • Elder Guardian
  • Warden
There are also 2 Modes you can play in Minecraft.
  • Singleplayer
  • Multiplayer

Overworld

The Overworld is the main dimension in Minecraft.
It is where players spawn and where most of the game takes place.
The Overworld is filled with various biomes, such as forests, deserts, and oceans, each with its own unique features and resources.

Nether

It is accessed through a Nether portal, which can be created using obsidian blocks and a flint and steel.
Portals do not work in the End, so players cannot travel between the Overworld and the End using portals.
The Nether is a dangerous dimension filled with lava, fire, and hostile mobs.
The Nether is home to unique resources, such as Nether Quartz and Glowstone, which are used in crafting and building.

End

The End is a mysterious dimension that can be accessed through an End Portal thats located in the stronghold.
To activate the End Portal, players must find and place Eyes of Ender, wich can be crafted with an Ender Pearl and Blaze Powder, in the portal frame.
It is home to the Ender Dragon, the final boss of Minecraft.
It is the only dimension where players can find End Cities and Elytra, which are used for flying.



Survival

In Survival mode, players must gather resources, build structures, and survive against hostile mobs.
Players have a health bar and hunger bar that must be managed to stay alive.

Creative

In Creative mode, players have unlimited resources and can fly.
This mode is often used for building and designing structures without the limitations of survival gameplay.

Adventure

Adventure mode is designed for custom maps and challenges created by other players.
Players cannot break blocks unless they have the appropriate tools or permissions.

Spectator

Spectator mode allows players to fly through blocks and observe the world without interacting with it.
Players are invisible to mobs and cannot be harmed in this mode.



Ender Dragon

The Ender Dragon is the final boss of Minecraft and is found in the End dimension.
Defeating the Ender Dragon is a major milestone in the game and rewards players with experience points and access to the End Gateway, which leads to the outer islands of the End.

Wither

The Wither is a powerful boss mob that can be summoned by players using three Wither Skeleton skulls and four soul sand blocks.
The Wither drops a Nether Star upon defeat, which is used to craft a Beacon.

Elder Guardian

The Elder Guardian is a large hostile mob found in ocean monuments.
It has a unique attack called the Mining Fatigue effect, which slows down players' mining speed.
Defeating the Elder Guardian rewards players with prismarine shards and crystals, which are used to craft prismarine blocks and sea lanterns.

Warden

The Warden is a blind, hostile mob that resides in the Deep Dark biome.
It is extremely powerful and can deal massive damage to players.
The Warden is attracted to vibrations and sounds, making stealthy gameplay essential when navigating its territory.



Singleplayer

In Singleplayer mode, players can explore and build in their own world without the presence of other players.
This mode allows for a more relaxed and personal gameplay experience.

Multiplayer

Multiplayer mode allows players to connect to servers and play with others online.
Players can collaborate, compete, and participate in various activities and mini-games on multiplayer servers.
There are a lot of servers with twists too like
  • Strength SMP
  • Lifesteal SMP
  • Glitch SMP
  • Bliss SMP
  • And many more



Strength SMP

Strength SMP is a Minecraft server that focuses on player-versus-player (PvP) combat.
Players can engage in battles, form alliances, and compete for dominance on the server.
If you get a kill you gain 1 Attack Damage and if you die you lose 1.

Lifesteal SMP

Lifesteal SMP is a unique Minecraft server where players can steal hearts by killing other players but if you die you lose a heart.
This adds an extra layer of strategy and excitement to PvP encounters.

Glitch SMP

Glitch SMP is a Minecraft server that embraces glitches and exploits within the game.
These are not actual glitches but are custom made by the server.
Players can experiment with various glitches to gain advantages or create unique gameplay experiences.

Bliss SMP

Bliss SMP is a Minecraft server that has special gems that harness abilities.
Players can work together to create impressive structures, participate in events, and enjoy a friendly multiplayer environment.
